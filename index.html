Before you turn it up

  • Start at a low volume. Sustained tones can damage hearing and speakers.
  • Ultrasonic content above ~20 kHz is inaudible but still real energy — tweeters can overheat.
  • Frequencies below ~20 Hz mostly move air, not sound. Most phone speakers reproduce neither end.
  • The usable top end is half the audio sample rate shown above (Nyquist limit).
